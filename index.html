The Origin

It started on Old La Honda.

Before there was a club, there was a group chat ranking each other on local Strava segments — Old La Honda in Woodside, and every other climb worth bragging rights. Skyline Velo Cycling Club turns that rivalry into something real: organized rides, a shared leaderboard, and a club that's open to any rider 18 and under.

Open recruitment. Every rider welcome.
