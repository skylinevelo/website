The Origin

It started on Old La Honda.

Before there was a club, there was a group chat ranking each other on local Strava segments — Old La Honda in Woodside, and every other climb worth bragging rights. Skyline Velo Cycling Club turns that rivalry into something real.

Pocket One · The Schedule

Two rides a week, built around school.

Wednesday evenings (the anchor ride) and Saturday long rides. Meeting points and exact times are still being finalized — check Instagram before you head out.

Pocket Two · Join

18 and under. Any ability. Free.

No dues, no tryout. Message us, get a parent or guardian to sign the waiver, show up ready to ride.
