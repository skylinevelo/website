Skyline Velo Cycling Club · Logo Concepts, round 2

The shield, the wordmark, and how they pair.

Round 1's icon mark and monogram are retired below — you're right that neither was it. New direction: Skyline Blvd is, officially, California State Route 35 — a real, specific, load-bearing fact about the club's actual road, not an arbitrary graphic. A route shield is also about as simple as a mark gets by design: flat, no gradients, built to be read at speed from a moving car. That solves "simpler" and "means something" at the same time.

The test: each mark shown in the club's colors, then reduced to one flat color — a mark that only works in full color isn't ready for a jersey or a cap.

Why this pairing: a wordmark for full lockups and a compact icon for tight spaces is a completely standard system (it's exactly what the site's current nav already assumes — a small circle mark plus the brand name). The 35 shield fills the "simple, small, on-brand" role you're describing; the wordmark keeps the full name legible when there's space for it.

Still not final — the arched "SKYLINE" text and the "35" weight/spacing are easy to tune once you confirm this is the right direction. If you want, I can also swap this straight into the website's nav so you can see it in place rather than in isolation.
