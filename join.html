Join the Club

18 and under. Any ability. Free.

There's no fee, no minimum fitness level, and no tryout to join Skyline Velo Cycling Club — just show up ready to ride. Here's what actually happens before your first ride:

1. Get in touch

Message us on Instagram or email admin@[domain] to let us know you're interested.

2. Get the waiver signed

A parent or guardian signs it — required for every rider under 18. See the waiver page for details.

3. Show up

Bring a helmet, water, and a rideable bike. Wednesday evenings and Saturdays — exact times and meeting points are still being finalized, so check Instagram before you head out.
